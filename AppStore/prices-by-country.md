# Prices by country

Three tiers by the World Bank's income groups (its fiscal-2027 lists, in `~/.claude/skills/aso/references/price-tiers.md`):
full price in the 69 high-income stores, about 40% off in the 53 upper-middle-income stores, and about 70% off in the 53
lower-middle- and low-income stores, Uzbekistan among them. Each tier's prices are Connect's comparable prices for a US price,
$1.79 / $11.99 / $23.99 and $0.89 / $5.99 / $11.99, so a store whose price includes tax pays a little more in dollars.

**The saving:** in every store the yearly plan saves 43–45% against twelve monthly payments, the figure the paywall
shows. Where Connect's comparable yearly price missed that range, the monthly price was kept and the yearly price set to
the store's price point nearest twelve monthly payments × 0.557, the US ratio. That moved the yearly price in 46 tier
stores and in 42 high-income ones: 29 of those went down, the euro stores to €19.99, and 13 went up, with existing
subscribers kept at their price. All 175 stores now save between 43.5% and 45.0%.

**What Apple no longer adjusts:** in the 106 tier stores the lifetime price was set by hand, and Connect says it won't
adjust a hand-set price for tax or exchange rates. The World Bank revises its lists every 1 July: review the tiers then.
